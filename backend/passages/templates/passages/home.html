Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an institute to use TypingSkillZone?

No. Students can register directly and practise on their own. Institutes use a separate workspace for managing students and staff.

Which languages are supported?

English and Hindi passages are available, in the layouts used by the exams listed above.

How are results calculated?

Speed and accuracy follow the scoring rules configured for each exam. Results for rules that are not yet verified are shown as practice results, not official qualifications.

What happens if my page reloads during a test?

Your typed text is saved every few seconds and restored when the page reloads. The timer continues, so keep an eye on the clock.

I did not receive my verification email. What should I do?

Check your spam folder first. You can ask for the email to be sent again from the registration page.
